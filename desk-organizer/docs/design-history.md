# Design history

- **Rev H**: original bedside organizer, notebook holder, phone stand, pen storage.
- **Rev I**: early three-slot concept; rejected because geometry differed from reference.
- **Rev J**: redesigned with slanted dividers, stepped heights.
- **Rev K**: equal-height dividers.
- **Rev L**: five dividers creating four slots.
- **Rev M**: dividers made fully vertical.
- **Rev N**: two pen cups instead of one.
- **Rev O**: Python/CadQuery parametric generator, STL and STEP.
- **Rev P**: scale to 20 mm slot clearance; 240 mm wide.
- **Rev Q**: seven dividers and six slots; 240 × 184 mm base.
- **Rev R (current)**: narrow X dimension to 200 mm to fit Creality K1; Y depth 184 mm; 20 mm slots retained.

All measurements in mm. Geometry is based on iterative design assumptions, not measured physical notebooks. Source of truth: `parameters.json`.
